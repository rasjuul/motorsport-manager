## Completed

- Driver name input, random base lap times from 80 to 90 seconds, race totals, winner or tie, and winning margin.
- Arrays recording both drivers' final lap times, fastest laps, and average times displayed with three decimals. Base lap times remain whole seconds.
- Three player pace choices: careful (+2 seconds, 5 tyre wear), aggressive (-2 seconds, 20 tyre wear), and normal (no pace adjustment, 10 tyre wear). Invalid pace input defaults to normal.
- Player pit stops restore tyres to 100 before pace and wear are applied, and add 12 seconds to that lap. Both y and Y are accepted.
- Tyre condition is clamped at zero. Wear is applied before the same lap's tyre penalty: +8 seconds at 20 or below, otherwise +4 at 40 or below.
- The rival uses normal pace, loses 10 tyre condition per lap, and receives the same tyre penalties. The rival's tyre condition is displayed.
